About

I am a first-year master's student in astrophysics at Masaryk University, focusing my research on tidal disruption events (TDEs) as a pathway to explain Little Red Dots. Guided by Dr. Isobelle Lilian Mary Garland and Dr. Michal Zajaček, I investigate how these rare transients illuminate galaxy evolution.

My academic journey began with exploring why only some radio galaxies form extended structures. I love translating complex astrophysics into public outreach, whether through telescope sessions or podcast storytelling.
